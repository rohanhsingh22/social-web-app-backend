2. COMPARISONS (spec tasks 9-13)
- Proportions: Regular Female (character-01 Aria) / Regular Male
  (character-02 Kai) selected; match existing fallback scale; shared
  Humanoid rig keeps animations/equipment compatible. Teen/Superhero
  rejected for launch.
- Head/hair: 20-style pack pool; launch pins 1 style per character in
  Phase 2. Eye/skin customization reserved (not launch scope).
- Outfit quality: v2.1 modularity fix de-risks clipping; casual/peasant
  pieces preferred over noble/wizard/knight (cozy social direction).
- Village style: cozy social village (paths, gardens, plaza, fountain,
  bridge, benches, lanterns). Castles/fortifications/weapons/dungeons
  explicitly rejected — must not read as medieval RPG.
- Character/world consistency: single creator (Quaternius), shared
  stylized low-poly aesthetic, shared Humanoid rig, warm daytime palette
  on both sides. No style clash expected; confirm visually in Phase 5.

3. PERFORMANCE FIT (targets, not measurements)
- ~13k tris/character x4 = well within mobile budgets; low-poly env;
  15 MB animation standard zip but only 6 clips ship (independently
  loadable); 4 shared prop texture sets limit state changes.
